Naturopathy

Alongside behavioral support, I am also interested in natural approaches that can contribute to your pet's overall well-being: its emotional balance, but also its everyday vitality.

These approaches never replace a veterinary diagnosis or treatment. They are used as a complement, with overall well-being in mind.

Discover the different approaches I offer:

  • Bach flowers — flower essences to support your pet's emotions
  • Trace elements — trace elements for vitality
  • Herbal medicine and supplements — plants and their benefits
  • Clay therapy — the virtues of clay
  • Nutrition — feeding your dog or cat well at every stage of life
  • Homeopathic emergency kit — the essentials to have before going on vacation
