Homeopathic emergency kit for dogs and cats

Which homeopathic remedies can I give my dog for stress? Homeopathy is sometimes used by dog and cat owners as a complement, especially in situations of occasional stress: going on vacation, moving house, a minor trauma, a passing digestive upset…

Here are some examples of remedies traditionally associated with these situations:

  • Stress, anxiety about change — Gelsemium, Nux vomica
  • Minor trauma, bumps, knocks — Arnica
  • Insect stings — Apis, Ledum palustre
  • Passing digestive upsets — Nux vomica
  • Car sickness — Cocculus
Important reminder

These remedies are mentioned for information only. Homeopathy never replaces veterinary advice, and the choice of remedy and its dosage must be decided with a professional (homeopathic vet or pharmacist) according to your pet's actual situation. If in doubt, if a symptom persists, or if the situation seems serious, always see your vet first.
