Herbal medicine and supplements for dogs and cats

Which plants can I give my dog or cat naturally? Some plants and natural supplements can support your dog's or cat's general well-being, as a complement to a balanced diet — never as a replacement.

  • Seaweed and algae (spirulina, chlorella, dulse) are rich in protein, minerals and antioxidants; they are often used as a course to support vitality, especially in older animals or during growth.
  • Brewer's yeast is a natural source of B vitamins, good for the skin and coat of dogs and cats.
  • Sprouted seeds (wheat, alfalfa, lentils) are rich in easily absorbed vitamins and minerals.
  • Turmeric, black cumin and thyme are spices traditionally used in small amounts in food for their recognized properties.
  • Flaxseed provides omega-3s, useful for coat and skin quality.
  • Nettle, parsley and horsetail are plants traditionally used for their richness in vitamins and minerals (parsley should be used in moderation).
  • Garlic, often mentioned in "natural" recipes, should be avoided: it is toxic to dogs and cats alike, even in small repeated amounts.
  • Pollen can be used to support general vitality, especially in older animals.
Important reminder

These natural supplements are always used in small amounts, as a course, and as a complement to a balanced diet. They never replace veterinary treatment. Always ask for advice before any supplementation, especially for a dog or cat that is ill, pregnant or on medication.
