Laurena Haroy – Dog and Cat Behaviorist

Welcome,

I'm Laurena Haroy, and I have been working as a dog and cat behaviorist since 2007. I help owners facing behavior problems — aggression, house soiling, separation anxiety, excessive barking or meowing, fears and phobias… — to better understand their animal and enjoy a calm, peaceful relationship again.

Consultations in French only

I offer in-person consultations in Essonne (south of Paris, France) and video consultations, in French only. The information on this site is available in English to help you better understand your dog or cat.

If you speak French: +33 6 20 65 78 88 – consultation page (in French).

I'm also the creator of the apps “Understanding My Dog” and “Understanding My Cat”, which give you a personalized emotional assessment of your pet, and the author of the books of the same name.
