Books to understand your dog's and cat's behavior

Alongside my apps, I have written books to help you understand your pet's behavior in greater depth, available on Amazon.

Understanding My Dog

Does your dog bark for no apparent reason, pull on the leash, or not come back when you call? This book on dog behavior offers a different approach: not a list of exercises to apply, but a clear and accessible way to understand your dog in depth, identify the real causes behind its behavior and adapt your responses to its profile.

View on Amazon

Understanding My Cat

Does your cat scratch the furniture, bite without warning, or relieve itself outside the litter box despite your care? This book on cat behavior helps you move away from correcting and toward understanding: decoding everyday behaviors, understanding what your cat really feels, and building a clearer, calmer relationship.
